THE NIGHT STRETCHED ENDLESSLY, WRAPPING THE WORLD IN A QUIET THAT FELT ALMOST DELIBERATE, AS IF EVERYTHING HAD AGREED TO PAUSE AT THE SAME MOMENT. SHADOWS LEANED INTO ONE ANOTHER, TRADING SECRETS ONLY DARKNESS COULD UNDERSTAND, WHILE THE AIR CARRIED A FAINT HUM OF THOUGHTS LEFT UNFINISHED. SOMEWHERE FAR AWAY, A PLACE WITHOUT A NAME CONTINUED TO EXIST SIMPLY BECAUSE IT ALWAYS HAD, INDIFFERENT TO TIME AND MEMORY. MOMENTS DRIFTED BY WITHOUT URGENCY, BLENDING PAST AND PRESENT INTO A SINGLE, SLOW BREATH. NOTHING DRAMATIC HAPPENED, YET EVERYTHING FELT IMPORTANT, LIKE THE CALM BEFORE A REALIZATION THAT NEVER FULLY ARRIVED.
